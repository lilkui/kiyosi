# Option pricing

Option values and their sensitivities under specified contract terms and
market conditions.

## Language

**Higher-order Greeks**:
Speed, charm, color, vanna, and zomma describe changes in delta or gamma as
spot, valuation time, or volatility changes.

**Speed**:
The change in gamma per unit change in the underlying asset's spot price.

**Charm**:
The change in delta as valuation time advances, expressed per calendar day.

**Color**:
The change in gamma as valuation time advances, expressed per calendar day.

**Vanna**:
The change in delta per percentage-point change in volatility.

**Zomma**:
The change in gamma per percentage-point change in volatility.
